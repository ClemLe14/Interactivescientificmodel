IMPORTANT: ARROW STYLE RULE
────────────────────────

A “double arrow” means:

• ONE single straight vertical line
• With one arrowhead at the top
• And one arrowhead at the bottom

It must NOT be two separate arrows.
It must NOT be two lines.
It must be one continuous line with two arrowheads.

A “single arrow” means:

• ONE straight vertical line
• With ONE arrowhead at the top

No triangular floating shapes.
No side arrows.
No horizontal green arrows.
